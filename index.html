Field notes · photo IDs, signal path & purchase checks

What makes a playable melody?

Route oscillator → filter → VCA → output. Pitch CV sets the note; a gate triggers the envelope controlling its loudness. Both Moogs supply this internally. The photographed 960 can sequence the Neutron without a computer. See Doepfer’s patch examples.

Why this suggested split?

Andrea gets the powered case for SWT16+ → Wave drums, Lollipop compression and bluebox recording. Her own West Pest can sequence a melody, and the Neutron adds a sweepable filter and analog delay. Parker gets both Moogs: keep them in their desktop enclosures to make layered, clocked melodies before buying a rack. The joystick, DeFeel, 960/962, 130 and other loose modules need that second powered rack.

What the dollars mean

Photo IDs refine capabilities, not condition or resale prices. Values are editable USD negotiation estimates shared across scenarios. The seller’s $500 DeFeel, $700 bluebox and “1k alone” around the Moogs are not completed sales. The cash adjustment assumes equal contributions to the purchase.

One included powered case

The suggested split assigns the wooden rack and power system to Andrea as one $400 item. Edit the card if you agree on a different used value. Parker’s future rack is a separate expense, not part of the listing or cash adjustment. Keep the Moog enclosures and matching power adapters with Parker; confirm both are present and working. The West Pest enclosure does not power Andrea’s loose modules.

Getting into TouchDesigner

Use a compatible audio interface → Audio Device In CHOP → Analyze CHOP for sound-reactive visuals. The A-174-4 sends CV, not USB. For slow gestures use a voltage-rated DC-coupled input or a CV-to-MIDI bridge. The bluebox’s CV inputs control its mixer; do not assume they stream raw CV.

Still worth testing

Photo 1 shows the Subharmonicon racked and Mother-32 outside the rack. Confirm included enclosures, power supplies and two usable output paths. Test the Neutron’s oscillator-mix shaft, joystick axes, Robaux outputs and Wave microSD playback. Check Lollipop’s revision and bluebox’s firmware and power requirements. The photos establish panel identities, not working condition.
